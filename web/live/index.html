From a step to a pattern

s("bd ~ sd ~") plays a kick, a rest, a snare, then a rest. The spaces divide the pattern into equal steps.

note("C4 E4 G4 ~") does the same with pitches. Press a numbered step to select and hear it without changing your score or moving its playback position. Choose a key to change its pitch, or a named drum choice to change its sound. Rest writes ~, a silent step.

The keyboard runs from lower notes on the left to higher notes on the right. Rest sits beside the Octave controls; for drums, it stays among the sound choices. Octave −/+ shows another range without editing the score; press a key to use it. While stopped or paused, sound choices automatically preview that step; choosing Rest stops the preview. During playback, edits follow their normal update timing. Press a numbered step whenever you want to hear it separately.

Undo and Redo live in the score toolbar and act on the whole score, including both typing and sound choices. Undo can restore a note replaced with a rest; press its numbered step to hear it again. Left/Right moves between steps, keys, or drum choices without editing; Space/Enter activates them. Use Tab to reach Rest beside Octave. On a step, Up/Down changes pitch by one semitone; Shift moves an octave. Escape returns to your code selection. Ctrl/⌘+Z and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z also work while using the sound controls.

bpm(96); sets the tempo. .slow(2) stretches a pattern to twice its length.

What the controls can edit

Controls edit plain sequences of up to 32 drum sounds or notes, including rests. Nested rhythms, repetitions, chords, and transformations remain fully editable as code; the controls show source steps, not the transformed playback timeline. Pitch nudges stay within MIDI 0–127.

A preview plays about 0.7 seconds of the selected phrase with just that source step, using the same DSP, declarations, and modifiers attached directly to the phrase. Other voices and modifiers on enclosing groups or later references are not included. Use Play to hear the complete result in context.

The small knobs edit gain and filter values for note and chord voices. Browser drums are not affected by those modifiers. Drag vertically, use arrow keys, or press Enter to type a number.

Controls hides or shows the pads without changing your music. Downloads are plain-text .mini files you can edit, share, or use with your own AI tools. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

While playing

Updates take effect at each material's next entry. Pending changes wait while the current phrase finishes. Invalid code leaves Current song unchanged.

Pause freezes the performance. Resume with Play, or use Restart to apply a new song structure from the beginning. Author tempo with bpm(90);; omitted tempo is 60 BPM.
